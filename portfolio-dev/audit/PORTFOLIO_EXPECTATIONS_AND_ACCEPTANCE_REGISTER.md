## Artistic team veto conditions — nonnegotiable before the next owner review

- **Photography:** No hero, spread or large image displayed above the resolution that makes JPEG edges, jaggies or softness objectionable in the actual rendered view. Record native pixel size, CSS displayed size, pixel ratio, photo sharpness and fallback design for any small original.
- **Layout:** No default single-image row or enormous text/image pairing for repeated projects. No multi-screen run of near-identical cards. The first viewport and each page transition must demonstrate multiple related images in a designed composition where appropriate.
- **Art direction:** Proof must be desktop/mobile screenshots of whole sections, not just source-code diffs or four top-screen thumbnails. Keep proven colors and approved brand assets; evaluate composition before adding decoration.
- **Biographical:** Keep nine chapters and detailed employer record as **source content**, but reject nine identical layout modules. No personal email/phone/address or downloadable employment résumé.
- **Critical reviewer:** A failed requirement is an explicit veto, not a challenge that can be overridden by passing automated browser tests. Record why any proposed design is rejected.
- **Release manager:** Before publication provide all four complete final pages, changes, confirmed exact assets and original-photo mapping, privacy scan, mobile/desktop QA, and explicit owner approval.
