# RRR identity — homepage and header integration study (2026-11-13)

## Design treatment
A separate, preserved HTML visual study was created from the user's **actual saved** gold full-signature candidate and the user's **actual saved** black-and-white traditional RRR monogram (recolored to flat warm gold strictly as a review copy; original artwork unchanged).

- Header: compact RRR monogram on dark emerald at left; clear four-page portfolio navigation.
- Home: wide gold-framed full signature gets one considered feature position, *not* a repeated permanent header component.
- Gallery: neutral ivory, two equal columns at desktop and single column on narrow phone; original project-photo URLs and contain sizing retained.
- No ornamental borders applied to photo cards; no crop or alteration of gallery originals.
