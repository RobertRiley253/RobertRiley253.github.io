## Selection standard

Prioritize demonstrable artistic improvement, factual provenance, authentic photos, dual audience readability, privacy, and sustainable navigation. Reject complexity that makes the site slower, less clear, less credible, or more invasive. Preserve the earlier advanced versions and obtain explicit approval before merging or publishing.
