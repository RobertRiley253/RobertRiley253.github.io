An eye for design.
A hand in the making.

My work spans commercial illustration, sign-business ownership, race-car sponsorships, hot-rod restoration and custom automotive graphics, B-2 aircraft manufacturing, graphite-fiber composites, exotic-metal welding, CNC machining, 3D printing and teaching.
