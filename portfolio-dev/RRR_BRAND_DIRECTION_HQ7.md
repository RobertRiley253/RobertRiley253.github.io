# RRR identity — owner-review directions (Production HQ7)

**Status:** Three conceptual directions; no owner approval, no release, no replacement of current provisional RR/ mark.

## Constraints
- Name: **Robert R. Riley**; monogram should read clearly as **RRR**, not RR/ or R/R.
- Work across a 36–44px masthead slot, small favicon, printed résumé masthead, portfolio cover, and one-color production application.
- Pair with the existing deep-forest, ivory and restrained copper design palette. Prioritize legibility, genuine signmaking craft and precision rather than decoration.
- Do not depict an aircraft, a certification seal or implied endorsement. Avoid stock clip art, generic gears and flashy gradients.

## Direction A — Precision letterform (recommended for review)
Three clearly legible uppercase Rs in a custom-spaced, compact wordmark. A single measured line or small copper rule may reference drafting and accurate layout, but must not become a fourth glyph. Can be drawn and scaled cleanly in one color. **Best match:** multidisciplinary creative and technical practice, timeless and readable.

## Direction B — Shop-maker signature
A restrained serif RRR lockup paired with the written name, with hand-crafted optical adjustments rather than a handwritten flourish. **Strength:** warmth and illustration heritage. **Risk:** fine strokes may collapse at mobile-navigation sizes; require a separate simplified small mark.

## Direction C — Fabrication stamp
Block-style RRR set in a framed, squared industrial shape suggesting an identification plate. **Strength:** strong production and equipment association. **Risk:** may overstate the industrial side at the expense of illustration and typography.

## Review sequence
1. Produce clear visible monochrome and forest/copper mockups of the three directions in the existing masthead dimensions.
2. Compare recognition at 36px and at favicon size, on light and dark backgrounds, plus print scale.
3. Owner selects, rejects or requests refinements; only then replace provisional identity in four pages and shared styles.
4. Run fresh automated cross-viewport QA after any brand implementation, then request final owner approval separately for publication.

**Do not infer brand selection from this direction document.**
