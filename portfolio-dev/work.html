ON THE TRACK / IN THE SHOP / ON THE CAR

I knew the race.
I made the graphics.

Competing in demolition derby, bump-to-pass, Thunder Car, figure-eight and short-track events gave me a direct connection to motorsports. My sign businesses also sponsored dozens of race cars, linking the demands of racing with the craft of lettering, custom paint and fabrication.
