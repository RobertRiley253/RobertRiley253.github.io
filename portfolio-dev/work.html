CUSTOM AUTOMOTIVE / RACING / GRAPHICS

Born in the shop.
Proven at the track.

Race cars and custom vehicles have been both a passion and a working canvas. I competed in demolition derby, bump-to-pass, Thunder Car, figure-eight and short-track racing, while my signage business sponsored dozens of race cars. That first-hand experience shaped how I think about graphics, appearance and durability.
